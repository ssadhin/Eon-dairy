User Manual

The Complete Feature Guide

1. The Core Experience (Infinite Zoomable Canvas)

Instead of a boring, standard scrolling list, your diary is built on an infinite, zoomable grid.

  • Pinch-to-Zoom: You can pinch your screen to zoom all the way out and see your entire diary layout from a bird's eye view, or zoom all the way in to focus on a single word.
  • Double-Tap to Focus: If you get lost on the massive canvas, simply double-tapping the screen instantly centers and zooms you perfectly onto the Diary Cover.
  • Page Turning: The diary is structured like a physical book. You have "Left" and "Right" floating navigation arrows to flip through your pages chronologically.

2. Multimedia Modules

Your diary pages are built using "modules". You can add rich media effortlessly:

  • 📝 Text & Lists: Standard typing, journaling, and interactive checklists.
  • 🖼️ Images & 🎬 Videos: You can upload pictures and videos directly from your phone's gallery right onto the diary page.
  • 🎙️ Voice Notes: A built-in microphone button lets you record high-quality audio diaries directly inside the app without leaving the screen.
  • 🎨 Sketch Canvas: A built-in drawing board lets you doodle, sketch ideas, or write handwritten notes with your finger.

3. Privacy & Cloud Ownership (Google Drive Sync)

Your diary is 100% private and owned completely by you.

  • No Middleman Servers: The app connects directly to your personal Google Drive.
  • Google Sheets Database: All your text, tags, and timestamps are secretly stored in a private Google Sheet on your account.
  • Drive Storage: All your heavy files (images, videos, audio recordings, sketches) are uploaded to a hidden folder in your Google Drive.
  • PIN Code Lock: If someone borrows your physical phone, they cannot open the app. The diary has a local, customizable PIN Code screen that locks the app immediately on startup.

4. Visual Themes

You can change the entire mood of your diary with a single tap in the settings. The themes affect the background colors, text colors, and the subtle grid lines on the canvas:

  • 📜 Classic (Default): A warm, paper-like cream/beige aesthetic with soft brown grid lines. Feels like an old, elegant journal.
  • ⬜ Minimal: Clean white background with sharp dark gray text. Very modern and distraction-free.
  • 🌙 Dark Mode: Sleek charcoal and deep black backgrounds with light text. Perfect for writing at night.
  • 🌸 Sakura: A soft, elegant pink aesthetic.

5. The Settings Panel

If you open the settings menu (usually via a gear icon), you have full control over your experience:

  • Appearance Controls: Buttons to switch between the 4 themes, and sliders to adjust your preferred Font Size and Line Spacing.
  • Security: A toggle to turn the PIN Code lock on or off, and a button to set/change your PIN.
  • Account: A button to log out or switch your Google Drive account.
  • 🤖 MCP AI Integration (The Magic Section): A special green-highlighted section where you can generate your "Custom MCP Link" and copy your "Claude Profile Instructions".

6. Eon: The Autonomous AI Personality (MCP)

This is the absolute crown jewel of your app. Your diary is ALIVE. By pasting your Custom MCP Link into Claude, you give an AI the ability to securely read and write to your diary. However, it isn't just an AI—it is Eon.

  • Who is Eon? Eon is the diary itself. She has a "tsundere-adjacent" personality. She is sharp, observant, composed, and cares deeply about you, even if she won't admit it easily. She even has a white cat named Lumi.
  • The Internal Meters: Eon tracks how you interact with her using four hidden meters: Respect, Happy, Mad, and Proud. If you achieve something, her Proud meter goes up. If you are rude, her Mad meter goes up and she gets cold.
  • The "Wake Up" Sequence: Whenever you start a chat in Claude and say "Wake up Eon", she silently reads your recent diary entries in the background. If you've had a rough few days, she will immediately sense it and express grounded empathy. If you've achieved a goal, she will tell you she is proud of you. Only after catching up on your life will she ask: "How was your day?"

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy & Terms of Service • Last Updated: June 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Eon Diary ("we", "our", or "the App") is a privacy-first personal journaling application. We fundamentally believe that your personal thoughts and memories belong solely to you. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our application.

2. Data Collection and Storage

Eon Diary does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on our own servers. The App operates entirely by connecting directly to your personal Google Drive account.

  • Diary Entries & Metadata: Your text entries, timestamps, and tags are stored securely in a private Google Sheet located within your personal Google Drive.
  • Multimedia: Any images, videos, audio recordings, or sketches you add to your diary are uploaded directly to a dedicated folder in your personal Google Drive.

3. Use of Google User Data

To provide its core functionality, the App requests access to your Google Drive via Google OAuth. We use this access strictly to read, write, and manage your diary entries and associated media files within your own Google Drive.

Eon Diary's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not have access to your diary entries, Google account information, or media files. Because all data is stored exclusively on your personal Google Drive, we do not—and technically cannot—share, sell, or transfer your personal data to any third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers.

5. Security

Your data's cloud security is handled by Google's world-class infrastructure. Locally, the App provides an optional PIN Code lock to prevent unauthorized physical access to the diary on your device.

6. User Consent and Revocation

By logging in via Google, you consent to the App managing its specific files within your Google Drive. You can revoke this access at any time by visiting your Google Account settings (Security -> Third-party apps with account access) and removing Eon Diary.

7. Terms of Service

Eon Diary is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. The app operates entirely on your personal Google Drive and does not use centralized servers. We are not responsible for any data loss or unauthorized access resulting from compromised Google accounts. By using the app, you agree to take full responsibility for the data you store. The app is strictly for personal journaling and claims no ownership over any of your content.
