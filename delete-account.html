Account & Data Deletion Request

Eon Diary • Developer: Asani Soft / Asani Studio BD

This page describes how users of Eon Diary (developed by Asani Soft) can request and execute the complete deletion of their account, email address, and all associated personal data.

1. Self-Service Account & Data Deletion (Instant)

Because Eon Diary does not store your journal data on developer servers and instead connects directly to your personal Google Drive account, you have immediate and total control over deleting your data at any time:

Step 1: Delete Local Data & Disconnect Account

  1. Open the Eon Diary app on your Android device.
  2. Go to Settings → tap Log Out / Disconnect Account. This erases the cached email address and session tokens from your device.
  3. Alternatively, open your device's Settings → Apps → Eon Diary → Storage & Cache → tap Clear Storage / Clear Data.

Step 2: Delete Stored Diary Entries & Media Files

  1. Open Google Drive in your browser or app using your Google Account.
  2. Locate the Eon Diary folder and the associated Google Sheet.
  3. Right-click or tap the options menu and select Delete / Move to Trash, then empty your Trash to permanently delete all journal entries, voice notes, photos, and sketches.

Step 3: Revoke App Permissions

  1. Visit your Google Account Third-Party Access Settings.
  2. Select Eon Diary and click Remove Access / Delete Connection. This immediately and permanently disables the app's OAuth token.

2. Types of Data Deleted vs. Kept

  • Email Address: Immediately removed upon logout, app data clearance, or uninstallation. No copy is kept on any external server.
  • Diary Entries, Notes & Media: Permanently deleted when you delete the corresponding files in your personal Google Drive. Zero retention by Asani Soft.
  • Retention Period: 0 days. We do not maintain backups or logs of your private data.
